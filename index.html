About me

I got into web development in Bangladesh, where I worked as a front end developer on more than 12 projects. Most of that work was landing pages, templates and setting up development environments for the team. In 2017 I moved to Japan, because I wanted to build my career in the IT industry here.

I studied application programming at Azuma Technical College. After I graduated I worked at Haneda Airport, where I also led a team, and later in mobile sales. Talking with customers all day taught me to listen properly and explain things in plain words. I still use that with teammates and clients.

Right now I am an IT engineer at Vuetech. My days are mostly PHP, Laravel, jQuery and Docker, plus testing, debugging and improving what we have built. I want to grow into a full stack engineer, so I keep learning and practicing regularly outside of work too.
